About Nordpore

Built on the KTH cleanroom infrastructure, Nordpore represents a breakthrough in nanopore manufacturing technology. We manufacture ultrathin, geometry-tunable solid-state nanopore chips and package them as ready-to-use flow cells.

We are dedicated to building an accessible and highly customizable nanopore platform that empowers researchers and industry partners to advance single-molecule analysis technologies. By combining precision nanofabrication with flexible design capabilities, we aim to accelerate discoveries in genomics, proteomics, and molecular diagnostics through collaborative innovation.

ANNOUNCEMENT October 16, 2025

KTH Innovation Grant

We are excited to announce that we received the KTH Innovation Grant to support the development of our ultrathin, geometry-tunable solid-state nanopore technology. This grant recognizes the innovative nature of our nanopores, enabling us to further advance our R&D efforts and making our pores available to the entire single-molecule sensing community.

We are grateful to KTH Innovation for their continued support in bringing cutting-edge nanotechnology from the lab to research communities, as well as making precision nanopore solutions accessible to researchers worldwide.
